- Helps reduce acne
- Removes dark spots
- Gives glowing skin
- Suitable for teenagers and young adults

### 🎯 Target Audience
- College students
- Young professionals
- People struggling with acne issues

### 📱 Platforms
- Instagram Reels
- YouTube Shorts
- Facebook Ads

### 🎨 Tone
- Authentic  
- Relatable  
- Conversational  
- Attention-grabbing

- Create hooks under 10 words

1. Hook
